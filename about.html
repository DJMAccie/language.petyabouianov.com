About Nihongo Studio

A free web app for learning everyday Japanese vocabulary, built around 84 themed word lists. It is made for two kinds of people: anyone heading to Japan who wants enough words to get around, and anyone who wants to grow their vocabulary without hunting for a word list first.

  • 84themed lists
  • 1,313words in the library
  • 4quiz modes
  • Freeno account needed

The word lists are already built

Finding a decent vocabulary list is normally the first job. That part is done. The library is grouped by theme rather than alphabetised, so a list is something you can finish and use: City and Transport, Restaurant & Bar, Travel Essentials, Body Parts, Numbers, Basic Question Words, and eighty more.

The useful words come first in each list. Starter Kit holds 74 words and covers the greetings, pronouns and polite forms that most sentences lean on. Travel Essentials and Restaurant & Bar are the two to open before a trip.

Every list is editable once you have an account. Delete what you already know, add words from your own reading, or split a large list into smaller ones. Your edits stay in your account and leave the shared library alone for everyone else.

Lessons come in fives

A lesson introduces five words. You see each word first and answer for it afterwards, so nothing is tested before it has been shown. Finishing a set brings up the next one, and the daily screen keeps track of how many sets you have cleared. Five is small on purpose. It takes a couple of minutes, and that is a far easier habit than an hour once a week.

Four answering modes cover the same words from different directions:

  • JP → EN shows the Japanese and asks for the meaning.
  • EN → JP gives the meaning and asks you to produce the Japanese.
  • Speech asks you to say the word aloud.
  • Multiple choice is the quick pass when you want speed over recall.

Kana and kanji are shown alongside romaji rather than instead of it, so you can read a word from the first day and pick up the script as you go. Words you get wrong come back sooner than words you know, and every word carries an accuracy figure, so you can see what is sticking. Typed answers accept romaji and Enter submits.

Review works on what you got wrong

The review queue holds words you have already met and not yet mastered. It puts the words that are due first, then the ones you have struggled with, so a short session goes where it is needed. Words leave the queue as you get them right consistently.

You can use it without an account

The whole app is open. No sign-up wall, no trial, no limits on which lists you can open. Without an account your progress is saved in your own browser, which is fine on a single device. Making an account keeps that progress, syncs it across devices, and lets you edit and create your own lists. Signing in with Google or Apple creates a separate account from a username and password, so pick one route and stay with it.

Accounts store your lists and progress and nothing else.

It is free, and donations are genuinely appreciated

Nihongo Studio is free in full. There is no advertising and nothing costs money. One person builds and pays for it, which covers the hosting and the time to keep the word lists and quizzes in good shape.

A donation is welcome and never expected. If the app has been useful and you want to put something back, it goes straight into keeping it running.

Where to start

Open the studio and work through Starter Kit, or jump straight to Travel Essentials and Restaurant & Bar if a trip is coming up. The library is on the Lists tab, the dictionary lookup and grammar notes are beside it, and a themed set of kanji packs sits behind the torii icon in the header.
