========================================================================
LISTS
========================================================================

PARAGRAPH
Wise men say that you can understand about 80% of conversations by knowing 20% of the words. So, by that philosophy I started researching what those words were and packaged them in easy-to-learn units. During the lessons with my sensei, each time I tried to say something and didn't know the word he wrote it down. So a lot of the lists are also based on natural vocabulary. I recommend you try to make those as well and add them as a list in the app. 
In this app you will find lots of basic lists as a starting point. 

THE THEME NAMES (ONE PER LINE)
City and Transport
Restaurant & Bar
Travel Essentials
Body Parts
Numbers
Basic Question Words

PARAGRAPH
Starter Kit, the first list, covers the greetings, pronouns and polite forms most sentences lean on. Travel Essentials and Restaurant & Bar are the two to start with before a trip.

PARAGRAPH
These are the lists I used, so you don't have to go looking. With an account you can edit them, split them, or make your own.
